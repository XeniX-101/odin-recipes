Nigerian Smoky Jollof Rice


Description

A beloved, classic West African dish. This flavorful one-pot rice is simmered in a rich, spiced tomato and red bell pepper sauce.

Ingredients

  • Long-grain parboiled rice
  • Fresh tomatoes, red bell peppers, and scotch bonnet peppers (blended)
  • Tomato paste
  • Onions (sliced and chopped)
  • Vegetable oil
  • Chicken broth (stock)
  • Curry powder, dried thyme, bay leaves, salt, and seasoning cubes

Steps:

  1. Heat vegetable oil in a large pot and sauté the chopped onions until translucent.
  2. Add the tomato paste and fry for a few minutes to remove the sour taste, then stir in your blended pepper mix.
  3. Season the sauce with curry, thyme, bay leaves, salt, and seasoning cubes, allowing it to cook down until the oil separates.
  4. Pour in the chicken broth and bring the mixture to a boil.
  5. Wash your parboiled rice thoroughly, stir it into the boiling sauce, cover tightly (using foil under the lid helps trap the steam), and cook on low heat until the rice is tender and the liquid is completely absorbed.
