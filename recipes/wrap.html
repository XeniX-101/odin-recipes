Chicken Wrap


Description

A quick, healthy, and highly customizable meal. Perfect for a busy weekday lunch or a light dinner.

Ingredients

  • Large flour tortillas (or flatbreads)
  • Cooked chicken breast (grilled or shredded)
  • Lettuce or mixed greens
  • Sliced tomatoes and cucumbers
  • Shredded cheddar or mozzarella cheese
  • Mayonnaise, garlic sauce, or honey mustard dressing

Steps:

  1. Warm the flour tortilla slightly in a dry pan to make it soft and easy to roll.
  2. Spread a thin layer of your chosen dressing across the center of the tortilla.
  3. Lay down a bed of lettuce, then layer on the sliced tomatoes, cucumbers, and chicken.
  4. Top with a sprinkle of shredded cheese.
  5. Tuck in the bottom and sides of the tortilla, roll it up tightly, and slice it in half to serve.
